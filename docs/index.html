MiniNav for ESP32-C6 AMOLED

A turn-by-turn display for the Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 (480×480). Your phone plans the route and sends each turn over Bluetooth; the board shows it.

What this is, and isn't

This board has no PSRAM (512 KB RAM), so it cannot decode Android Auto's video. This version shows turn arrows, distance, street name, speed and ETA instead of the Google Maps picture. Routing comes from OpenStreetMap, not Google Maps.

Step 1 · Install the firmware

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Install, choose the board's serial port (it shows as USB JTAG/serial) and wait about a minute.
  3. If it doesn't connect, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, and try again.

Troubleshooting

    

      
Wrong chip errorThis installer only accepts an ESP32-C6.

      
Black screen after flashingUnplug and replug USB. Check the board is the AMOLED-2.16 model.

      
MiniNav not in the Bluetooth listPress RESET on the board. The board only advertises while no phone is connected.

      
Search or routing failsThe phone needs internet (mobile data is fine). The free OSM services can be busy, so try again.
